Welcome to the CreditBook Help Section! Here, you’ll find instructions on how to use CreditBook effectively. Follow the steps below to understand how to create an account, take credit, and manage payments.

How to Use CreditBook

  • Creating an Account: Visit the signup page and fill in the required details, including your username, email, and password. Once submitted, you’ll be redirected to your dashboard.
  • Scanning the QR Code: Open the app, navigate to the scan option, and point your device’s camera at the merchant’s QR code. The app will detect the merchant’s username automatically.
  • Entering the Amount: After scanning the QR code, enter the amount you wish to take as credit. Confirm the transaction to notify the merchant.
  • Taking Credit: Once the merchant approves your request, the amount will be added to your credit history, visible in your dashboard.
  • Payment Approval/Denial: Merchants can choose to accept or reject a payment request. Accepted payments will reflect in the customer’s credit record.
  • Mark as Paid Button: After a customer settles their credit, the merchant can mark the transaction as “Paid.” This action updates the credit record and removes the transaction from outstanding balances.
