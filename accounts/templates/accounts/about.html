About CreditBook

CreditBook is a digital platform designed to streamline credit management between merchants and customers. It enables merchants to maintain accurate, real-time digital records of credit transactions, allowing customers to track their credit history effortlessly.

Featuring QR code integration, user-friendly dashboards, and advanced analytics, CreditBook simplifies and enhances the traditional credit recording process, making it seamless and transparent for both parties.

New & Enhanced Features

  • Merchants can now access data of customers willing to take credit from them.
  • Merchants can view their total credit taken from the market and the amount repaid.
  • Customers receive a credit score based on their repayment behavior.
  • Analysis of how long customers take to return credit.
  • Month-wise credit analytics with interactive graphs.
  • Pie chart visualization of total credit taken vs. total amount repaid.

Key Features

  • Digital credit transaction management.
  • Real-time credit record updates.
  • QR code integration for effortless transactions.
  • User-friendly dashboards for merchants and customers.
  • Automated analytics for better credit tracking.
  • Customer scoring system for responsible lending.
  • Comprehensive reports exportable in Excel format.
